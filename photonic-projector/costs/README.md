# Projector Cost Register

The CSV is a cost-collection template, not a priced bill of materials. No reliable total can be calculated until the 8K SLM, RGB laser modules, optical prescription, throw range, cooling design, safety classification, and video interface are selected.

## Cost categories to obtain quotes for

1. Native 8K SLM and timing electronics.
2. RGB laser sources, drivers, beam combining, and monitoring.
3. Illumination and polarization optics.
4. Projection lens and mechanical focus/zoom assembly.
5. Video receiver, frame buffer, FPGA, and high-speed interconnect.
6. Chassis, thermal management, vibration isolation, and alignment.
7. Laser safety enclosure, interlocks, shutters, and professional review.
8. Metrology, calibration, assembly, and environmental testing.

## Estimating policy

- Keep supplier quote currency and date.
- Record part number, lead time, minimum order quantity, and whether taxes/shipping are included.
- Separate engineering NRE from recurring unit cost.
- Add packaging, spares, calibration, service, import duties, and contingency.
- Never fill unknown component costs with invented values.
