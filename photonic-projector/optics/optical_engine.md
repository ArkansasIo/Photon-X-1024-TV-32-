# Projector Optical Engine — Concept Note Rev 0.1

## Candidate arrangement

- Red, green, and blue laser illumination channels.
- Beam combining optics and polarization conditioning.
- Integrating rod or equivalent homogenizer for uniform illumination.
- Reflective LCoS SLM candidate, subject to confirmation of native 7680 × 4320 resolution and 120 Hz operation.
- Projection lens with focus and zoom/throw requirements derived from enclosure and screen geometry.
- Optical power monitoring and fail-safe laser shutter.

## Optical design tasks

1. Select wavelengths based on SLM, coatings, colour gamut, safety, and supplier availability.
2. Determine illumination numerical aperture and pupil matching.
3. Model polarization and PBS losses for the selected LCoS architecture.
4. Model pixel pitch, diffraction orders, lens MTF, and screen sampling.
5. Design speckle reduction without compromising image stability or safety.
6. Specify coatings, filters, thermal limits, and alignment tolerances.
7. Verify accessible emission and interlock design with a qualified laser-safety review.

## Required measurements

- Spectral output and wavelength stability.
- Optical power at the image plane.
- Uniformity and colour registration.
- Contrast and black level.
- MTF across centre and corners.
- Speckle contrast under defined observation conditions.
- Focus drift during thermal soak.

This document is a design starting point. It is not a manufacturing drawing or a laser-safety certification.
