# Audio Verification Plan

## AUD-VT-01 — Digital input
- Verify supported PCM sample rates and bit depths.
- Confirm channel mapping and correct sample-rate transitions.
- Inject packet loss, clock interruption and silence; verify controlled recovery without loud pops.

## AUD-VT-02 — Electrical performance
- Measure DAC and amplifier frequency response, THD+N, SNR, crosstalk and output noise.
- State load impedance, output power, bandwidth and measurement weighting for every result.
- Confirm no clipping at the specified continuous and peak output levels.

## AUD-VT-03 — Acoustic performance
- Measure frequency response at the listening position and across a defined listening window.
- Measure maximum SPL, distortion, driver excursion and enclosure vibration.
- Evaluate dialogue intelligibility, stereo imaging and bass response using controlled listening tests.
- Verify grille, cabinet and stand do not introduce rattles.

## AUD-VT-04 — A/V synchronization
- Measure audio-to-video offset using a synchronized flash/click test.
- Repeat at every supported display refresh and video processing mode.
- Verify adjustable delay range and resolution.

## AUD-VT-05 — Protection and thermal
- Test startup mute, shutdown mute, DC fault, short-circuit, over-current and over-temperature behavior.
- Run sustained output tests while monitoring amplifier, power supply and enclosure temperatures.
- Confirm fault handling cannot damage drivers or expose users to unsafe temperatures.

## Exit criteria
Publish measured results only after repeatable tests with documented equipment, calibration, loads, microphone position and environmental conditions.
