# Photonics Cost Data

`mpw_reference_prices_2026.csv` records published multi-project-wafer (MPW) reference prices checked 2026-10-31. It is not a complete project budget.

## Published reference points

- AIM Photonics lists non-member standard MPW offers at USD 34,320 for a 25 mm² passive PIC (20 chips), USD 42,900 for an 8 mm² active PIC (20 chips), and USD 54,000 for an 8 mm² Quantum Flex PIC (20 chips).
- EUROPRACTICE's 2026 schedule lists imec iSiPP50G silicon-photonics MPW at EUR 11,000 for a quarter block, EUR 22,000 for a half block, and EUR 44,000 for one block. The listed dimensions and delivery terms must be checked against the current process documentation before budgeting.

## Exclusions / quote-required items

The MPW figures do not establish the total cost of a working accelerator. Budget separately for design engineering, PDK access or membership where applicable, packaging, fiber attach, edge couplers, laser, modulators/drivers, phase-shifter control, photodetectors, TIAs, ADC/DAC, FPGA, thermal control, test fixtures, characterization, shipping, duties, taxes, and contingency.

## Currency and estimate policy

- Preserve the supplier's quoted currency (USD or EUR); do not combine currencies without a dated exchange rate.
- Treat published list prices as reference prices, not purchase quotes or guaranteed availability.
- No total project cost is asserted until the die architecture, PDK, packaging route, quantity, and instrumentation requirements are selected.
